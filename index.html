About the project

THE STORY OF INK BOTTLE

Some Secrets Should Stay Sealed.

Deep within a forgotten laboratory, five mysterious bottles sit among ancient books, strange instruments, and traces of forgotten magic.

No one knows exactly what lies within each bottle. Every vessel holds a different secret, waiting for the right moment to be discovered.

The first bottle has finally been revealed. But four others remain sealed, their identities hidden behind glass and violet mist.

Built on INK Chain, INK BOTTLE begins as a collection shaped by mystery, individuality, and the excitement of discovering what comes next.

This is only the beginning. Not every secret is ready to be told.
